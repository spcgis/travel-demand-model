Origin-Destination Trip Through Oakland - Fifth Ave. Contraflow Lane Closure Analysis

Use the filter panel on the right-hand side to select a scenario. The base map shows where Oakland through-trip are coming from. Click on a TAZ to select it as the origin. The map will then update to show where through-trips from the selected zone are going to.

A through-trip in this analysis is defined a trip passing through Fifth or Forbes Avenue but does not have an origin and destination zone in Oakland.
